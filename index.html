Understanding
Mycetoma

Mycetoma is a chronic, progressive, and severely debilitating infectious disease affecting skin, subcutaneous tissue, and bone. Classified as a neglected tropical disease, it disproportionately strikes the world's most vulnerable communities — those with limited access to diagnosis and care.

10+ Years of Work
~8,600 Global Cases Reported
70+ Countries Affected
What is Mycetoma?

Caused by fungi (eumycetoma) or bacteria (actinomycetoma), infection typically occurs through trauma — a thorn prick, a barefoot wound. Without early diagnosis, it leads to severe disfigurement and often amputation.
